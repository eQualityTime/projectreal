*   You tell your friend that someone was impersonating them. They are surprised, but don’t think it’s a big deal.
*   You don’t want to make a big deal out of it either, so you just block the number and move on.
*   😬 A few weeks later, another friend gets tricked and loses money.
*   🤔 Lesson: Just because you didn’t fall for it doesn’t mean others won’t. Blocking protects you, but reporting scams helps protect others too.

*   You realise that the fake profile copied your photos and details from your public profile.
*   You switch your account to private so scammers can’t steal your photos and details.
*   You go through your old social media posts and your tagged photos and delete anything that reveals your personal information.
*   You remove any suspicious accounts that you don’t actually know.
*   You also turn on two-factor authentication to make sure NO one can hack your real account.
*   🎉 Great decision! By taking all these measures, you have protected yourself. Now, it’s much harder for anyone to impersonate you again.
*   🤔 Lesson: A private and secure account means fewer risks of impersonation.

*   You don’t reply again, thinking they’ll stop messaging.
*   😬 A few days later, they message again, this time with a different story – now they claim they lost their wallet and need help!
*   🤔 Lesson: Ignoring a scammer doesn’t stop them. In fact, scammers might try the same people again and again to see if they’ll give in. Blocking and reporting stops them from trying again.

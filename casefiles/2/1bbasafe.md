*   You keep chatting to them online.
*   You chat about things like hobbies and realise you both play football.
*   Their team recently stopped training, so you invite them to join yours.
*   Over time, you become friends in person through football in a group setting. 
*   Looking back, you both agree it was smart to be cautious at first. 
*   🤔 Lesson: Meeting through shared activities is a great way to get to know someone safely. Taking your time and setting boundaries helps build genuine friendships while staying safe.

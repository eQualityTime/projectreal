*   You don’t message them again.
*   A few weeks later, someone else asks for your number. This time, you recognise the warning signs. 
*   🎉 You made a safer choice. You don’t have to stop talking to people online – JUST  know how to stay safe.
*   😬 But then you hear that the same person started messaging other people, trying to get their numbers too.
*   🤔 Lesson: If somebody changes how they communicate with you after you set boundaries, it is possible that they did NOT have good intentions. Blocking and reporting them can stop them from targeting others. Some people also make multiple accounts to keep trying to connect, so the other account messaging could be the same person too. Reporting them can get them removed completely.

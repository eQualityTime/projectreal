*  You realise the conversation is making you uncomfortable and tell a trusted adult like a parent or teacher.
*   You block the number.
*   You also review your privacy settings and make sure that NO personal and contact information is visible on your profile.
*   A few days later, the player’s account disappears from the game. 
*   🎉 You made a safer choice! If the player deleted their account after you set a boundary, it is possible they did NOT have good intentions. Or MAYBE  they were removed from the game for breaking rules. 
*   🤔 Lesson: If something feels off, it’s okay to step away. Talking to a trusted adult can help you act responsibly.

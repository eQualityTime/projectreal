*   You try to change the subject when they ask personal things.
*   They seem okay with it at first.
*   But then they start getting annoyed. They ask if you’re really friends, why don’t you tell me where you live?
*   😬 You enjoy chatting to them about the game, but don’t want to answer personal questions and feel uncomfortable being pressured. 
*   🤔 Lesson: If someone is pressuring you to share personal details, that’s a 🚨 red flag! Real friends respect your boundaries. Maybe it was a risky decision to share your WhatsApp number and keep talking when they started asking personal questions.

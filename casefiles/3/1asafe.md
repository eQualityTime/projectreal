*   You give them your number and they message you on WhatsApp. 
*   You really enjoy chatting about the game more. 
*   You use WhatsApp to arrange times to meet up and play the new release. 
*   The person turns out to be JUST  a regular gamer, and you become good online gaming friends. 
*   🤔 Lesson: This time, everything was fine, but it’s always good to think before sharing your personal number. Consider whether you really need to move the conversation to another platform and what topics feel safe to discuss. Keeping chats game-focused and avoiding personal details helps protect your privacy.

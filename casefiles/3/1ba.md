*   You don’t click the link, but you also don’t do anything else.
*   A few days later, the same account messages you again, this time with an even scarier warning. They say your account will be permanently deleted in 24 hours if you don’t act NOW.
*   Lesson: Scammers often try again if they think you might fall for their tricks. Ignoring is better than clicking, but reporting the scam is even smarter.

*   You decide to keep chatting but avoid meeting in person.
*   They get upset that you do not want to meet up and blame you for being paranoid. 
*   Over time, they start messaging you more, getting upset when you don’t reply quickly. They even start trying to call you regularly.
*   They ask you to prove that they can trust you by sharing a secret and sending photos.
*   The messages make you feel uncomfortable. You don’t want to answer their questions. 
*   Lesson: If someone gets upset about your boundaries, that’s a 🚨 red flag. You don’t owe anyone your time, trust, or personal information.

*   You decide to report the player to the game’s moderators.
*   A few days later, the player’s account disappears. They were removed for breaking the rules.
*   🎉 Great decision! You didn’t just protect yourself, you helped protect others too!
*   🤔 Lesson: It’s okay to step away if something feels off. If somebody gets upset when you set a boundary or when you refuse to share personal information, that’s a 🚨 red flag and it might be a good idea to report them. This isn’t just about protecting yourself – it helps keep online spaces safer for everyone.

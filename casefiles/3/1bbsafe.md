*   You repeat that you are NOT comfortable sharing personal details including your personal number but are happy to keep chatting on the game. 
*   Now they understand why you didn’t want to move to WhatsApp.
*   They respect that and don’t push you for your number or any personal details. 
*   Over time, you continue playing together, and they remain a fun online friend. 
*   🤔 Lesson: Setting boundaries online is important. A real friend will respect them without making you feel bad. Keeping conversations within the game can be a good way to enjoy online friendships while staying safe. But what if they hadn’t respected your boundary? How would you have responded?

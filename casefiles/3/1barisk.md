*   They seemed fine the first time, so you agree to meet alone next time.
*   This time, they are more forward and ask personal questions – where you live, what time you’re usually home, if your parents work late. 
*   Now you feel uncomfortable. You don’t want to answer their questions. You make up an excuse and leave.
*   Lesson: Even if someone seems safe at first, you still don’t really know them. Some people who do NOT have good intentions may try to build trust first.

*   You start feeling bad and think that maybe you were too harsh, so you decide to message again and send them your number.
*   They reply immediately and then message you on WhatsApp.
*   😬 They start asking personal questions. This time, they are much pushier.
*   🤔 Lesson: If someone stops talking to you just because you set boundaries, maybe they weren’t really a friend in the first place. If they come back when you lower your boundaries, that can be a 🚨 red flag. Real friends respect your boundaries.

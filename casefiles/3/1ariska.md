Hello 

* The conversations start to feel more personal, and they begin asking for selfies or voice messages.
* You find out that they’re a bit older than they said at first. Then they start asking to meet up in person.
* 😬 You feel uncomfortable that they lied about their age. You do not want to share personal information, photos, and voice messages or meet up. 
* 🤔 Lesson: It’s easy for people to lie online. If you catch someone lying, if something feels off or if people ask you for personal information or ask to meet, that could be a 🚨 red flag! Even small bits of personal information can help strangers find out more about you than you realise.

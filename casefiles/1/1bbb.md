*   After reporting the scam, you decide to review your own account security.
*   You enable two-factor authentication, making it much harder for someone to hack your account.
*   You also change your account password to something stronger – one that’s different from any other accounts you use.
*   Great decision! You’ve taken real action to protect yourself from future scams!
*   Lesson: Scam prevention isn’t JUST  about avoiding scams! It’s also about making sure your accounts are as secure as possible. What else could you do to keep your personal information safe online?
